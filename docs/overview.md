# Overview

## Purpose
Maintain an authentic, consistent LinkedIn presence through scheduled AI-assisted content and engagement, then learn from results.

## Intended user
The account owner: a professional building a personal brand around AI product design, AI tools, and AI careers.

## Workflows
| Workflow | Schedule | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| A: Content briefing and publishing | Mon, Wed, Fri at 10:00 AM | Research, write, illustrate, publish, reply, notify |
| B: Weekly engagement report | Monday at 9:00 AM | Analyse the last two weeks and recommend actions |

Note: Workflow A is described as a "daily content briefing" but runs only on the three publishing days.

## Principles
Authenticity over volume; no invented facts; story-first writing; fixed schedule.
